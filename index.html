Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 4, 2026  |  Last Updated: May 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GlowUp Ledger, operated by GaWaPa Wholesale LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application. By using GlowUp Ledger, you agree to the practices described in this policy. Please read it carefully.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create a GlowUp Ledger account, we collect:

  • Your name or display name
  • Email address
  • Password (stored securely using industry-standard encryption via Supabase)

Self-Care Tracking Data

As part of the core functionality of GlowUp Ledger, we collect the information you voluntarily enter, including:

  • Mood logs and emotional wellness entries
  • Daily habits and self-care routines
  • Personal notes and journal entries
  • Progress tracking data

Your self-care data is personal and sensitive. We never sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it for any purpose other than providing you with the GlowUp Ledger experience.

Payment Information

If you subscribe to GlowUp Ledger Premium ($6.99/month), payment is processed securely through Stripe. We do not store your credit card number, CVV, or full payment details on our servers. Stripe's privacy policy governs the handling of your payment information and can be found at stripe.com/privacy.

Device Permissions

With your explicit permission, GlowUp Ledger may access:

  • Camera and Photo Library — to allow you to upload profile photos or images to your self-care entries
  • Location Data — to provide location-relevant wellness content or features. You may disable location access at any time in your device settings
  • Push Notifications — to send you reminders, wellness prompts, and app updates. You may opt out at any time through your device notification settings

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Create and maintain your GlowUp Ledger account
  • Deliver the features and functionality of the app
  • Process and manage your Premium subscription through Stripe
  • Send you app-related notifications and reminders (with your permission)
  • Respond to your support requests and communications
  • Improve and debug the application
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not use your data for targeted advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

GlowUp Ledger is built on Supabase, a secure cloud infrastructure provider. Your data is stored on Supabase's servers with the following protections:

  • Encrypted data transmission using HTTPS/TLS
  • Secure password hashing — we never store passwords in plain text
  • Row-level security policies limiting data access
  • Regular security monitoring and updates

While we implement industry-standard security measures, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account.

4. Third-Party Services

GlowUp Ledger integrates with the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase — backend database and authentication (supabase.com/privacy)
  • Stripe — payment processing (stripe.com/privacy)
  • Apple App Store / Google Play Store — app distribution platforms

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers — with trusted third parties (Supabase, Stripe) who assist in operating our app, under strict confidentiality obligations
  • Legal Requirements — if required by law, court order, or government authority
  • Business Transfers — in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred with prior notice to you
  • Your Consent — in any other circumstance, only with your explicit permission

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Access and Correction

You may access and update your account information at any time within the GlowUp Ledger app settings.

Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the email below. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

Notification Controls

You may enable or disable push notifications at any time through your device settings.

Location Access

You may revoke location permissions at any time through your device settings without affecting core app functionality.

Camera and Photo Access

You may revoke camera and photo library permissions at any time through your device settings.

California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion of your data, and opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your rights, contact us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

GlowUp Ledger is a wellness application designed primarily for women and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete such information.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or legitimate business purposes (such as fraud prevention or financial records).
